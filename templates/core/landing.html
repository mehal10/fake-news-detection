NLP Technology

Classic and modern natural language processing techniques power the system.

Text Preprocessing

Lowercasing, stop-word removal, stemming/lemmatization, and punctuation handling to normalize input for models.

Feature Extraction

TF-IDF vectorization and bag-of-words representations capture important lexical signals for classification.

Machine Learning Models

Supervised classifiers such as Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, and Random Forest for robust predictions.
